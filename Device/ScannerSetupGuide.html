1. Symbol CS4070

The CS4070 connects via MFi (Apple accessory protocol over SSI). It pairs through the iPad's Bluetooth settings and is not tied to a single app. — Official Manual (PDF)

Pairing to iPad

1 Turn on the scanner.
2 Press and hold the Bluetooth button until the scanner enters pairing mode.
● Single beep  ● Blue light flashing rapidly
3 On the iPad, open Settings → Bluetooth. The scanner appears as CS4070:xxxx under Other Devices — tap it to pair.
● Status shows "Connected" on iPad

Connecting in the App

4 Open your app and wait a moment for the scanner to connect.
● Scanner beeps  ● Blue light slows to a slow pulse  ● Green scanner icon in app toolbar

Using with Multiple Apps

The CS4070 works with multiple apps. Put the current app into the background and open another — the scanner hands off automatically, as long as each app disconnects the scanner when it goes to the background.
If the scanner doesn't hand off automatically: Force-close the current app. This releases the scanner immediately so another app can connect.
CS4070 and CS6080 conflict: If both scanners are paired to the same iPad, the CS4070 always takes priority and the CS6080 will not connect. Remove the CS4070 from iPad Bluetooth settings to use the CS6080.

2. Zebra CS6080

The CS6080 (model WMWW) connects via MFi and is app-associated — it pairs to one app at a time through an in-app alert, not through iPad Bluetooth settings. — Official Manual (PDF)

Avoid the CS6080 model VZWW (Keyboard Wedge). This variant does not use the scanner SDK and requires a different setup. Many third-party Bluetooth barcode scanners offer the same keyboard wedge functionality at a significantly lower price.

Pairing to iPad

1 Go to Settings → Bluetooth and remove any other Zebra or Symbol scanners that are already paired. Having multiple scanners paired at the same time will prevent the CS6080 from connecting.
2 Press the scan button on the CS6080 and wait 5–10 seconds to wake it up.
3 Open your app and wait up to 10 seconds. A pairing alert will appear automatically — tap Pair.
● Pairing prompt appears in app
4 Force-close the app and relaunch it. The scanner reconnects automatically.
● One long beep  ● Green scanner icon in app toolbar

Switching to a Different App

Because the CS6080 pairs to one app at a time, switching apps requires re-pairing:

  1. Go to Settings → Bluetooth and forget the CS6080.
  2. Turn the scanner off and back on.
  3. Open the new app and wait for the pairing alert — tap Pair.
  4. Force-close and relaunch the app. The scanner connects with a long beep.

3. Troubleshooting

Zebra Scanner Control App

Install Zebra Scanner Control from the App Store. It can help pair your scanner, test scans, and diagnose connection problems independently of your main app.

Scanner Not Appearing in Bluetooth

Make sure the scanner is in pairing mode — the blue light should be flashing rapidly. If the scanner appears in Bluetooth but won't connect, tap Forget This Device and try pairing again from scratch.

Green Icon Not Showing in App

Force-close and relaunch the app. If the scanner still doesn't connect, turn it off and back on, wait 10 seconds, then reopen the app.

CS4070 Not Releasing to Another App

The app must disconnect the scanner when it goes to the background for the CS4070 to hand off. If the scanner stays locked to one app, check that the app supports scanner background disconnect.
