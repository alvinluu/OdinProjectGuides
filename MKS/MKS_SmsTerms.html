SMS Terms of Service

These terms describe the My Kids Spending text messaging program: what messages we send, how to opt in and out, and how to get help.

Program Description

My Kids Spending sends transactional text messages to account holders who opt in, including order confirmations, order-ready/pickup alerts, low-balance alerts, and account verification codes.

How to Opt In

You can opt in inside the My Kids Spending app by entering your mobile number and agreeing to receive text messages. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your account activity.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt Out

Reply STOP at any time to cancel. You will receive a confirmation text and no further messages.

Help

Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at support@mykidsspending.com or (855) 302-0070.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Your information is handled according to our Privacy Policy: https://mykidsspending.com/PrivacyPolicy
