1. Getting Started

Inventory Tracker is an iPad and iPhone app for managing physical inventory counts. It connects to your Odin cloud server to download items, track stock changes via barcode scanning, and import updated counts back to the cloud.

App Tabs

TabPurpose
TransferDownload inventory, import to cloud, change area/department, email reports, access user guide
InventoryBrowse items, scan barcodes, filter & sort, view activity log and import history
SettingsConfigure scanner hardware, stock counting behaviour, and scan detection

Step 1 — Register Your Device

Before you can download inventory, your device must be registered with Odin Inc. The app generates a unique serial number automatically.

When you open the app for the first time in Cloud mode, it checks whether your device is already registered:

  • If your device is already registered, the app will automatically download your inventory and display a "Your device is registered and ready to use!" notification. No further action is needed.
  • If your device is not yet registered, a Registration Required banner appears at the top of the Transfer tab with a "Register This Device" button.

How to Register

1 Tap "Register This Device" in the Transfer tab.

2 The Register Device screen shows your device's Serial Number and device name.

3 Tap "Send Registration Email". The app sends your device details directly to support@odin-inc.com via the Odin server — no mail account required on this device.

4 Wait for Odin Inc. to provision your device.

5 Once notified, return to the Transfer tab and tap "Download Inventory". After a successful download, the Registration Required banner disappears permanently.

The serial number is unique to each device and does not change. You only need to register once per device.

Step 2 — Download Inventory for the First Time

1 Go to the Transfer tab.

2 Make sure Cloud mode is selected under Server Mode.

3 Tap "Change" next to Area and select your sales area.

4 (Optional) Tap "Change" next to Department to limit the download to a specific department. Leave it set to All Departments to download everything.

5 Tap "Download Inventory" and confirm when prompted.

6 Wait for the progress bar to complete. The app will download all items and load department and area names automatically.

Downloading inventory replaces all items currently on the device. Make sure any pending stock changes have been imported before downloading again.

2. How to Use the App

This section walks you through the complete day-to-day workflow — from choosing your server mode to submitting a finished count. Follow these four steps each time you do an inventory count.

Step 1 — Choose a Server Mode

Before anything else, make sure you are in the correct server mode. Open the Transfer tab and look at the Server Mode picker.

ModeUse when…
CloudYour establishment uses the Odin web application. All downloads, imports, and barcode management happen directly with the cloud database. Recommended
Local (Legacy)Your establishment uses the Windows Store Manager application. Inventory is sent back via email for manual import on the PC.
Switching server modes will require you to re-download your inventory, as the two modes draw from different databases.

Step 2 — Download Inventory

Inventory must be downloaded to the device before you can start counting. Go to the Transfer tab.

Cloud Mode

1 Under Location, tap "Change" next to Area and select the sales area you will be counting. Each area holds a separate inventory — selecting the wrong area will download the wrong items.

2 (Optional) Tap "Change" next to Department to limit the download to a specific department. Leave it set to All Departments to download everything in that area.

3 Tap "Download Inventory" and confirm when prompted. A progress bar shows the download status.

4 Once complete, the area and department names update automatically. You are ready to count.

Area selection is only available in Cloud mode. In Local (Legacy) mode, the area is entered as a number in the Area field.

Local (Legacy) Mode

1 Inventory must be exported from Store Manager first (see Section 15). Anyone with access to Store Manager can do this.

2 In the Transfer tab, enter your Area number and (optionally) a Department code.

3 Tap "Download Inventory".

Downloading inventory replaces all items currently on the device. Import or save any pending changes before downloading again.

Step 3 — Update Your Count

With inventory downloaded, switch to the Inventory tab to scan and update stock counts.

1 Open the Inventory tab. All downloaded items are listed here.

2 Scan a barcode using a connected hardware scanner, or tap an item in the list to open its detail page and adjust the count manually.

3 On the Item Detail page, use the +/− buttons to adjust stock, or type a count directly into the New Count field. Each scan increments stock by the item's increment value (default: 1).

4 Use the filter icon in the Inventory tab toolbar to filter by Edited status to see which items have been changed, or by Not Edited to see what is still outstanding.

5 Repeat until all items are counted.

The Edited badge appears next to any item whose count differs from the original downloaded value, making it easy to track your progress.

Step 4 — Submit Your Count

When counting is complete, return to the Transfer tab to send your counts back to the system. The method depends on your server mode.

Cloud Mode — Option A: Import Stock to Cloud Recommended

This is the primary method for Cloud users. It lets you review, select, and confirm exactly which items are imported before anything is written to the cloud.

1 In the Transfer tab, tap "Import Stock to Cloud".

2 The Import Preview screen opens. Select your import mode:

  • Replace — Sets cloud stock to your scanned count. Use for a full physical inventory count.
  • Receive — Adds your scanned count to the current cloud stock. Use when receiving a shipment.

3 Tap "Load Cloud Stock" to fetch current cloud values and compare them side-by-side with your scanned counts.

4 Review the list. Tap the circle next to each item to include or exclude it. Tap an item to edit its count before importing.

5 Tap "Review (n)" to open the dry-run preview. Fill in a Reference (required), and optionally an Order Number, Receive Date, and Email address for a PDF receipt.

6 Tap "Confirm & Import". Cloud stock is updated immediately and a receipt is emailed if requested.

For a detailed walkthrough of the Import Preview screen, including dry-run, filtering, and cost review, see Section 6.

Cloud Mode — Option B: Upload to Cloud

This method uploads your counts to a staging area in the Odin web application, where you can review and finalise the import from a larger screen.

1 In the Transfer tab, tap "Upload Inventory". A progress bar shows the upload status.

2 Once complete, log in to the Odin WebApp on your PC and navigate to Inventory > Sync to review and finalise the import.

For the full WebApp sync process, see Section 11.

Local (Legacy) Mode — Email Your Count

In Local mode, counts are sent back to the PC via email for import into Store Manager. Go to the Transfer tab and use one of the two email options under Email Options:

OptionHow it worksUse when…
Email via OdinSends inventory data through the Odin server — no mail account needed on this device. RecommendedThe device does not have a mail account configured, or you want a simpler one-tap send.
Email CSVSends a CSV file attachment using the device's built-in mail app.You need the raw CSV file for custom processing.

After the email is received on the PC, anyone with access to Store Manager can import it. See Section 16 for the PC import steps.

3. Transfer Tab

Device Registration (First-Time Setup)

When the app launches for the first time in Cloud mode, it automatically tries to connect and download inventory. If your device has not been provisioned yet, a Registration Required banner appears at the top of the Transfer tab.

  • Tap "Register This Device" to open the registration screen, which shows your unique device serial and offers a one-tap email to Odin Inc.
  • Once Odin Inc. registers your device, return here and tap "Download Inventory". The Registration Required banner disappears permanently after the first successful download.
If you already had inventory downloaded before, this banner will never appear — the app only shows it until the very first successful download.

Server Mode

Choose between Cloud and Local (Legacy) mode.

  • Cloud — Syncs directly with the Odin cloud database. Supports barcode management, import preview, dry-run, PDF receipts, and area selection. Recommended
  • Local (Legacy) — For establishments using a Windows application with a local database. Inventory is exported via email (CSV or Odin service) for manual import into Store Manager.
Switching modes requires re-downloading your inventory because the data comes from different databases.

Location

  • Establishment — Your organisation name, determined by device registration with Odin Inc.
  • Area (Cloud mode) — The sales area you are counting in. Tap "Change" to browse and select.
  • Department — Filter downloads to a specific department. Tap "Change" to browse. Select "All Departments" to download everything.

Downloading Inventory

1 Tap "Download Inventory".

2 Confirm when prompted. This replaces all items currently on the device.

3 Wait for the progress bar to complete. The app validates your serial, downloads items, saves them, and loads department and area names.

Downloading wipes all existing progress on the device. Make sure you have imported any pending changes before downloading again.

Zero All Stock

Sets every item's stock count to zero. Useful for starting a fresh physical count from scratch.

Upload Inventory (Cloud Mode)

Tap "Upload Inventory" to upload your inventory counts to the cloud staging area. A progress bar appears above the button showing the upload percentage. The button is disabled while an upload is in progress. From there, you can review and manage the import through the Odin WebApp Sync page. This is useful when you want to review or edit items on a larger screen before finalising the import.

Import Stock to Cloud (Cloud Mode)

Tap "Import Stock to Cloud" to open the Import Preview screen. This lets you review scanned inventory counts and import them directly to the cloud database without needing the WebApp. See Section 6 below for the full import workflow.

Email Options (Legacy Mode)

  • Email CSV — Emails inventory counts as a file attachment using the iPad's mail app for import into Store Manager.
  • Email via Odin — Emails inventory counts through the Odin server without needing a mail account on this device.

User Guide

Tap "User Guide" at the bottom of the Transfer tab to open this document as an in-app full-screen PDF viewer. Inside the viewer:

  • Email icon (top left) — Emails the guide PDF to your saved email address.
  • Share icon (top left) — Opens the system share sheet to share the guide link.
  • Done (top right) — Closes the viewer and returns to the Transfer tab.

4. Inventory Tab

Item List

Shows all downloaded items with:

  • Department colour bar on the left
  • Item name and department name
  • Barcode (if assigned)
  • Current stock count
  • Edited indicator when stock differs from the original downloaded count, with the change shown (e.g., "10 → 15, Added 5")
  • Import timestamp if the item was recently imported to cloud

Search

Type in the search bar to filter by item name or barcode. If Keyboard Wedge Mode is enabled, the search bar stays visible and auto-focuses for scanning.

Filters

Tap the filter icon in the toolbar to open the filter sheet:

FilterOptions
Status (pick one)All • Not Edited • Edited
Order By (pick one)Department • Name • Last Modified • Last Imported
No BarcodeShow only items missing a barcode
DepartmentSelect one or more departments to show

Active filters display as a summary banner at the top of the list with a "Clear" button.

Toolbar Buttons

  • Import List (left) — Opens import history showing past import batches.
  • Generate Barcodes (Cloud mode) — Batch-generate random barcodes for items without one. Choose digit length (4–20).
  • Filter (rightmost) — Opens the filter & sort sheet.

Activity Log

The most recent activity appears below the filter banner. Tap it to open the full Activity Log, which records every scan, stock edit, download, import, and barcode change with timestamps.

5. Item Detail

Tap any item in the list (or scan its barcode) to open the detail view.

Basic Info

  • PLU — Product lookup code (server identifier). Read-only.
  • Barcode — Current barcode. In Cloud mode, you can set, edit, generate, or scan a new barcode.

Stock Counting

  • Original Count — The count from when inventory was downloaded (read-only).
  • New Count — Your adjusted count (editable). Shows the change amount in green (gain) or red (loss).
  • Quick Adjust (+/−) — Buttons that add or subtract by the item's increment value.
  • Reset Stock — Restores count to the original downloaded value.

Increment

  • Increment Value — How much stock increases each time the barcode is scanned (default 1).
  • Adjustable from 1 to 100 with a slider, or type a custom value.

Barcode Management (Cloud Mode)

Cloud mode only. Barcode registration, editing, and generation are not available in Local (Legacy) mode. Switch to Cloud mode in the Transfer tab to enable this feature.

Items downloaded from the cloud may or may not already have a barcode assigned. When an item has no barcode, you can register one using any of the methods below. Once registered, the barcode is synced to the server immediately and saved locally — no separate upload step is needed. From that point on, scanning that barcode anywhere in the app will identify and open the item.

Registering a barcode on an item with no barcode

  • Type manually — Enter a barcode number in the text field and tap Save Barcode.
  • Generate Random — Tap "Generate Random Barcode" to create a unique random number with your chosen digit count. Saves and syncs immediately — no confirmation needed.
  • Scan with Camera — Tap "Scan Barcode with Camera" to open the back camera and scan a physical label. The scanned value saves and syncs immediately — no confirmation needed.
  • Hardware scanner — If a Zebra, Linea, or keyboard wedge scanner is connected and the item has no barcode, scanning registers the barcode directly. Saves and syncs immediately.

Editing an existing barcode

Tap "Edit" next to the current barcode. The old barcode is shown for reference while you enter or scan the new one:

  • Type manually — Enter the new barcode in the text field.
  • Camera icon — Opens the camera to scan a physical label. The scanned value fills the field but does not save automatically — tap Save Barcode to confirm.
  • Hardware scanner — Scanned value fills the field. Tap Save Barcode to confirm.
  • Tap Cancel to discard the change.
Barcode conflict: If the barcode you enter is already assigned to a different item, the app will show an alert and the barcode will not be saved. Choose a different barcode.
Use the No Barcode filter in the Inventory tab to quickly find all items that still need a barcode assigned.

6. Importing to Cloud (Full Workflow)

This is the complete process for sending your scanned stock counts to the Odin cloud.

First-time users will see a collapsible "How to Import" guide at the top of the Import Preview screen. After your first import, this guide collapses automatically. Tap it to expand again at any time.

Step 1: Choose Import Mode

ModeWhat it doesUse when
ReplaceSets cloud stock to your scanned countFull physical inventory count
ReceiveAdds scanned count to current cloud stockReceiving a shipment

Step 2: Load Cloud Stock

Tap "Load Cloud Stock" to fetch current values from the cloud. This shows a three-way comparison for each item:

Current (cloud) → Scanned (device) = Adjusted (final)

Step 3: Filter & Select Items

  • Use the Filter & Sort button to narrow the list (Changed / No Change, Imported / Not Imported, zero stock, no barcode).
  • Tap the circle next to each item to select or deselect it.
  • Use Select All / Deselect All to toggle the entire visible list.
  • Tap an item to edit its stock count before importing.
  • Swipe left on an item to remove it from the import entirely.

Step 4: Review (Dry-Run)

Tap "Review (n)" to see a preview of what will be written to the cloud.

  • Fill in the Reference field (required, max 25 characters) — a short description of this import.
  • Optionally enter an Order Number and set the Receive Date.
  • Enter an Email address to receive a PDF receipt.
  • Review item quantities and costs. Tap an item to edit its cost.
  • Tap the circle to exclude individual items.

Step 5: Confirm & Import

Tap "Confirm & Import" to execute the import.

  • A progress bar shows the upload status.
  • Cloud stock is updated immediately.
  • A PDF receipt is emailed if an email was provided.
  • All imported items are logged to Import History.
After import:
  • Replace mode — Device stock syncs to the final cloud value.
  • Receive mode — Device stock resets to 0, ready for the next receive cycle.

7. Import History

Tap the "Import List" button in the Inventory tab toolbar.

Note: Import history is only recorded when you import stock using "Import Stock to Cloud" in the Transfer tab. Imports made through the Odin WebApp do not appear here. If the list is empty, no in-app imports have been completed yet.

Navigating Batches

  • Stores up to 5,000 records. One batch is shown at a time.
  • Use the ‹ › arrows in the navigation bar to move between batches. The label shows "Batch N of Total", most recent first.
  • Each batch header displays: date/time, import mode, item count, receive date, reference, and order number.
  • Items in the batch are listed below with row index, quantity change, final stock, and department.

Viewing the PDF Receipt

  • Tap "PDF" in the top-left toolbar to open the receipt for the current batch in a full-screen viewer.
  • Inside the PDF viewer:
    • Email (top left) — Resends the PDF receipt to your saved email address.
    • Done (top right) — Closes the viewer and returns to the history list.

8. Barcode Scanning

Keyboard Wedge Mode

For scanners that send keystrokes (most USB and Bluetooth scanners).

  1. Go to Settings → enable Keyboard Wedge Mode.
  2. In the Inventory list, the search field auto-focuses.
  3. Scan a barcode — the app looks up the item automatically.
  4. If "Increment Stock" is enabled, stock increases by the item's increment value.
  5. The app navigates to the item detail page.

Zebra / Linea Scanner

For Zebra handheld devices or Linea sled hardware with dedicated scanner modules.

  1. Go to Settings → enable Use Linea Scanner.
  2. The app connects to the scanner SDK automatically.
  3. Scans are received in the background regardless of which view is active.

Camera Scanner

Available in Item Detail (Cloud mode) for scanning barcode labels with the device camera.

If a scanned barcode is not found, the app shows a "No item found" error and logs a "Scan Miss" to the activity log.

10. Tips & Best Practices

  1. Always download fresh inventory before starting a new count.
  2. Use the "Edited" filter to quickly find items you have changed.
  3. Load Cloud Stock during import preview to verify your counts against the current cloud values.
  4. Use Receive mode for shipment receiving — stock resets to 0 after import.
  5. Use Replace mode for physical inventory counts — cloud stock is set to your scanned count.
  6. Always tap "Review" before final import to preview what will be sent.
  7. Check the Activity Log if you are unsure what was scanned or changed.
  8. Lock the email address in Settings to prevent accidental changes.
  9. Use shorter barcodes (4–8 digits) for faster scanning and search.
  10. Import History lets you re-view and re-send PDF receipts for any past import.

11. Odin WebApp — Sync Inventory After Upload

This section covers the Odin WebApp (accessed via a web browser on your PC), not the iOS app. This applies to both Cloud and Legacy mode users who need to sync uploaded inventory on the web.

Accessing the Sync Page

  1. Go to the Odin WebApp at https://msdata.co/StoreManagerWeb
  2. Log in with your credentials.
  3. Navigate to Inventory > Sync.

Sync Inventory Page

This page shows a copy of what has been uploaded from the Odin Inventory iOS app. From here you can review and import the uploaded inventory counts into the Odin web system.

Filtering Options

  • Sales Area — Select the sales area to view uploaded items for.
  • Remove items with 0 stock count — Filter out items that were not counted.
  • Remove items with no barcode — Filter out items without a barcode assigned.
  • Order by item name / barcode — Choose your sort order.
Using the filter checkboxes will reset any manual modifications you have made to the displayed list. The underlying uploaded data is not affected.

Editing Before Import

You can modify the displayed data before importing:

  • Click Edit next to a row to change the stock count value.
  • Click Delete next to a row to remove it from the import list.
  • These modifications only affect the displayed copy — the original uploaded data is not changed.

Previewing & Generating Changes

1 Select Preview Receive or Preview Replace:

  • Preview Receive — Adds the scanned count to the current stock. Use this when receiving new items or a shipment.
  • Preview Replace — Sets the stock to match the scanned count. Use this when doing a full physical inventory count.

2 Click "Preview" to generate the changes.

3 Fill in the Reference field — a short description so you can look it up later.

4 (Optional) Fill in the Order Number if you have an invoice number.

5 Click "Generate" to create the inventory changes.

6 Review the generated table showing Item, PLU, Date, Quantity, Total Cost, Reference, Sales Area, Vendor Code, and Dept Code.

7 If everything looks correct, click "SAVE" to post the changes to the system.

After clicking SAVE, the inventory changes are applied permanently. Double-check the preview table before saving.

13. Odin WebApp — Where to Find Area & Department Codes

This section explains where to find the area and department codes used when downloading inventory. These codes are managed in the Odin WebApp.

Area Code (Sales Area Number)

  1. Log in to the Odin WebApp at https://msdata.co/StoreManagerWeb
  2. Go to Inventory > Sales Areas.
  3. The Sales Area numbers are listed (e.g., 2, 3, 4, etc.).
  4. In the app, enter the matching area number when downloading inventory.

Department Code

  1. Log in to the Odin WebApp.
  2. Go to Department / GL Code.
  3. Click "Detail" below a department to see its code.
  4. In the app, enter this code in the Department field to filter your download to just that department. Leave it blank to download all departments.

14. Desktop Setup — Store Manager (Legacy / Local Mode)

Sections 14–16 apply to establishments running the Local (Legacy) server mode with Store Manager on a Windows PC. Anyone with access to Store Manager can perform these steps. If your establishment uses Cloud mode, you can skip these sections.

Requirements

  1. A copy of WebOrder.cfg must be in the Shared folder (for local database connectivity).
  2. The portable.authenticate table must contain an entry for the device.

Installation & Device Registration

1 On the iOS device, open the Inventory app and go to Settings. Note the Serial number generated by the app.

2 On the PC, register the serial in the portable.authenticate table following the format of existing entries.

SQL reference: SELECT * FROM portable.authenticate WHERE uid LIKE '%inv' ORDER BY lastauth DESC;

3 Assign a unique UID name with the suffix "Inv" to identify this serial as an Inventory app serial (no separate license is required).

4 The device is now registered. The user can download inventory using the app's Transfer tab.

Developer Notes

  • Store Manager v. 1.3.2.114 (8/25/2014): iPod/iPad devices must be added to the portable.authenticate table, otherwise it tries to create Palm files.
  • An apostrophe in the descript1 field of an item, or blank descriptions at the end of the file, can cause the export to loop.

15. Desktop — Export Inventory to iOS Device

This section explains how to push inventory data from Store Manager on your PC to the iOS device for counting.

Export from Store Manager

1 In Store Manager, click Utilities > Portable Inventory.

2 Select Export.

3 Select iOS Scanner.

4 (Optional) Check Quantity if you want to send the current quantity to the iPod/iPad.

5 Leave Full POS unchecked.

6 Pick the Sales Area to export.

7 Click Process.

8 Confirm the overwrite message when prompted.

Download on the iOS Device

After the export completes on the PC:

  1. Open the Inventory app on the iPad/iPod.
  2. Go to the Transfer tab.
  3. Fill in the Area number (2 to 10).
  4. (Optional) Fill in Dept with the department code to filter by department.
  5. Tap Download Inventory.
  6. (Optional) Tap Zero All Stock to set all counts to zero before counting.

Finding Your Area Code

In Store Manager, open View/Edit Inventory. At the top, click on Sales Area and check the area code shown as Alt+X (where X is your area code number).

16. Desktop — Import Inventory Back to PC

After counting inventory on the iOS device, use one of these methods to get the data back to Store Manager.

Sending from the iOS App

In the Inventory app's Transfer tab (Local/Legacy mode), there are two export options:

OptionDescription
Email CSVUses your device's email account to send the inventory file as a CSV attachment. You will need to place the file in the Portable directory on the PC.
Email via OdinSends inventory data through the Odin mailing system on your behalf. Recommended

Importing on the PC (Store Manager)

1 In Store Manager, click Utilities > Portable Inventory.

2 Locate your Inventory_list.txt file in the Portable Directory.

3 Select one of the import options:

  • Import Quantities — Replaces the stock quantity based on Inventory_list.txt. Use this if you checked Quantity when you exported.
  • Adjust Stock — Increments the stock quantity based on Inventory_list.txt. Use this if you did NOT check Quantity when you exported.

4 Check iOS Scanner and PLU.

5 Select the same Sales Area that was used during export.

Make sure you select the correct import option (Import Quantities vs Adjust Stock) based on how you originally exported. Using the wrong option will result in incorrect stock counts.
